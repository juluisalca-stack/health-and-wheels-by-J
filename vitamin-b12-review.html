What is vitamin B12 for?

Vitamin B12 helps keep blood and nerve cells healthy and helps the body make DNA. These are roles of the nutrient, not a promise of benefits from this product.

Nutrition, without the hype

Adults generally need 2.4 mcg of B12 per day; during pregnancy, 2.6 mcg. This product is labeled 500 mcg. A daily nutrient requirement differs from a supplement dose.

Who may need extra attention?

Older adults, vegans and people with absorption problems may need to pay closer attention to B12. A clinician can help decide whether a supplement and this dose suit you.

Follow the label and ask a doctor or pharmacist about your needs, especially during pregnancy or when taking medicines. General information, not medical advice.
