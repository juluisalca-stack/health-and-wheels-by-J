What is lion’s mane?

Lion’s mane is an edible mushroom with shaggy white spines. It is used in East Asian cooking and sold as powders and extracts. A mushroom eaten as food and a concentrated supplement are different products.

What does the research say?

Findings about immunity, inflammation, blood sugar and nerves are largely from laboratory or animal research. They do not establish that this product treats diabetes or regenerates nerves in people.

Reading the bottle

The front says “3000 mg equivalent.” This is an equivalence claim, not a verified amount of extract per softgel. Check Supplement Facts and serving directions before choosing it.

Before you choose

Possible side effects include stomach discomfort, nausea and rash. Talk with a clinician before use, especially with diabetes or medicines. This is not a substitute for prescribed treatment.
